## What it is
Your primary home is the dwelling where you live most of the year. For retirement planning it is usually both your biggest expense and your biggest asset. The question is whether to keep it, right-size to something cheaper, or move closer to the water or family.

## Compare three paths at the same horizon
- **Stay:** taxes, insurance, repairs, utilities and any accessibility work.
- **Right-size:** a smaller home or a unit with a tenant. [Hermit Crab](animal:hermit-crab) compares buying a [duplex](resource:duplex-house-hacking), building an [ADU](resource:adu) or keeping the current home.
- **Rent first:** a one-year lease before buying. [Common Loon](animal:common-loon) and [Green Sea Turtle](animal:green-sea-turtle) compare renting, buying now and buying later.

## Selling: the home-sale exclusion
If you sell your main home, you may exclude up to **$250,000** of gain, or **$500,000** if you file jointly. You generally must meet both the **ownership test** (owned the home at least 24 months of the last five years) and the **use test** (lived in it at least 24 months of the last five years). You generally cannot use the exclusion if you excluded gain from another home in the previous two years. Do not show the gross sale price as spendable cash: subtract the loan payoff, selling costs and any tax.

## New York property tax relief
The STAR program can reduce school taxes for owner-occupied primary residences, with an Enhanced version for eligible seniors. Register when the home becomes your primary residence. Also ask your local assessor about senior exemptions.

## Tapping home equity
A reverse mortgage (the federally insured HECM) lets an owner aged 62 or older borrow against the home without monthly mortgage payments. HUD-approved counseling is required, the home must be your primary residence, and the loan grows over time. Treat it as a last-resort tool and compare alternatives first.

## Waterfront considerations
Price flood insurance, wind deductibles and elevation separately. See [Waterfront and flood insurance](resource:waterfront-flood-insurance).

## First step
Get a written estimate of every cost for each path, then bring the numbers to a HUD-approved housing counselor before you commit.
