## What it is
NYSLRS administers two systems: the **Employees' Retirement System (ERS)** and the **Police and Fire Retirement System (PFRS)**. ERS covers most New York State and local government employees outside the teacher systems. Your benefit depends on your **tier** (set by your date of joining) and your specific **plan**.

## Key facts from NYSLRS
- **Vesting:** Tier 1 through 4 members with five years of credited service are vested. Since April 9, 2022, Tier 5 and 6 members also vest at five years of service credit (previously ten).
- **Vested benefit:** once vested, you are eligible for a pension even if you leave public employment. You can apply later, and the benefit is based on the service and salary you earned.
- **Full retirement age:** 62, or 63 for Tier 6. Most members can retire as early as 55 with a reduction that depends on tier and plan.
- **Without reduction:** some members can retire before full retirement age with 30 years of service, or under a special plan that allows retirement after 20 or 25 years.
- **Withdrawing contributions:** Tier 5 and 6 members with five to ten years of service may withdraw their contributions instead, which ends their membership. That option disappears at ten years.
- **Applying:** vested members must apply, 15 to 90 days before the retirement date. To retire you must have a bona fide termination and be off the payroll.

## The comparison that matters
Request two official estimates at the same claim age: one assuming you keep working, and one with service and earnings frozen at the date you leave. The gap between them is the real cost of leaving early. A full-career estimate is not a substitute for a midcareer exit. That is the heart of [Pink River Dolphin](animal:pink-river-dolphin).

> Part-time credit, reductions and employer retiree health rules are separate checks. Ask NYSLRS and your employer for each one in writing.

## Bridging the gap
If you leave before the pension starts, you need income for the months in between, plus health coverage. [Spider Monkey](animal:spider-monkey) maps the final paycheck, end of coverage, [457(b)](resource:governmental-457b) eligibility and first pension payment on one calendar.

## Taxes
New York generally treats public pension income favorably for state income tax, but rules vary by year and by other kinds of retirement income. Do not extend a New York exemption to all retirement accounts, and check the rules of any state you move to.

## Find your tier and plan
Sign in to Retirement Online and open My Account Summary. You can also check your Member Annual Statement or ask your employer. Then read your plan book on the NYSLRS publications page.
