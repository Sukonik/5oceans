## What a counselor does
A HUD-approved housing counselor is a trained, independent adviser. They can:
- Review your budget, credit and savings and tell you what you can afford.
- Explain loan types and help you compare Loan Estimates.
- List grants and down-payment programs for your county.
- Provide the homebuyer education that many programs require.
- Help if you are behind on a mortgage or considering a reverse mortgage.

## Why start here
Programs differ by county, income and household, and the rules change. A counselor sees the options for where you live. They do not sell you a loan.

## How to prepare
1. Gather pay stubs, tax returns, bank statements and a list of debts.
2. Pull your credit reports and note your scores. Use the [Credit Card Tool](page:credit/index.html).
3. Bring the numbers from the [Real Estate Calculator](page:tools/real-estate/index.html#home).
4. Write down your questions.

## Beware of scams
Real counseling is offered by HUD-approved agencies. Be wary of anyone who:
- asks for a large upfront fee to "find you a grant",
- guarantees approval,
- tells you to sign documents you do not understand,
- pressures you to act today.
