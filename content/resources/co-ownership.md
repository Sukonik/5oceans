## What it is
Shared ownership means two or more people hold title to a home, such as siblings sharing a family lake house. [Capybara](animal:capybara) runs a missed-payment season and a buyout.

## Decide the structure
Common ways to hold title include joint tenancy, tenancy in common, and ownership through an LLC. They differ in what happens when an owner dies, whether shares can be sold, and how liability works. A real-estate attorney can explain which fits your family. This site does not give legal advice.

## Write down
- **Title and debt:** who is on the deed and who owes the mortgage.
- **Money in:** each owner's share of purchase, taxes, insurance, repairs and reserves.
- **Use:** an occupancy calendar and rules for guests.
- **Rental:** whether rental is allowed and how income and tax are split.
- **Exit:** how an owner sells, how the price is set and what happens if they cannot pay.

## Stress tests
- **Missed season:** one owner cannot contribute. Can the reserve cover it? What is the consequence?
- **Buyout:** the surviving owners' monthly cost after buying someone out. Can they afford it?
- **Death or divorce:** how a share passes.

## Taxes
Track fair-rent days, personal-use days and each owner's share of income and expenses. See [Vacation or second home](resource:vacation-home).
