## An investment is not an account
A Roth IRA, a governmental 457(b) or a taxable brokerage account is an **account arrangement**. A bill, bond or fund is something the account may hold. Track the account's access rules separately from the investment's market and liquidity risks.

## Choosing by when you need the money
| Vehicle | Good for | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Insured bank savings | Cash available immediately, for example after a layoff | Use the FDIC EDIE tool for coverage by bank, depositor and ownership category |
| Treasury bill ladder | Spending that starts on known dates | Bills mature in 4 to 52 weeks. Match maturities to the months bills are due. Selling early may change proceeds |
| Series I savings bonds | A reserve part you can leave untouched for at least a year | Locked for 12 months. Redemption before five years normally forfeits three months of interest |
| TIPS | Inflation-linked principal for a known later need | Compare maturity with the spending date. Market value can fall before maturity. Check taxation of annual principal increases |

## Keep immediate cash separate
Leave the first month or two of expenses in insured cash. Build the ladder behind it. [West Indian Manatee](animal:west-indian-manatee) tests access to insured savings during the first week of an emergency.

## Match the ladder to the plan
- [Black Bear](animal:black-bear) knows the months income stops and schedules bills by month.
- [Blue Whale](animal:blue-whale) plans a full year away and compares short bill maturities with the return move.
- [Sailfish](animal:sailfish) funds a trial stay with a return date and compares refundable cash with a bill that matures before the lease payment.

## Cautions
- Newly purchased I bonds should not be counted as first-year emergency cash.
- A bill ladder does not grow with inflation unless you reinvest at higher rates.
- Interest may be taxed. Treasury interest is generally exempt from state and local income tax, but verify for your situation.
