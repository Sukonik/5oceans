## What it is
Renting means paying a landlord for housing and not owning the property. It keeps cash flexible and lets you test a place without buying.

## When it fits
- **Trying a city:** [Sailfish](animal:sailfish) funds a 90-day trial stay with a return date.
- **Trying four seasons:** [Common Loon](animal:common-loon) compares a one-year lease with buying, including winter utilities and closing costs.
- **Coming back to your coast:** [Green Sea Turtle](animal:green-sea-turtle) compares renting first, buying now and buying later.
- **Keeping an exit:** [Great White Shark](animal:great-white-shark) prices the lease exit, travel and three months back home.
- **Moving between places:** [Wandering Albatross](animal:wandering-albatross) compares travel with a fixed base.

## Costs to compare
Rent, utilities, renter's insurance, parking, moving, security deposit, and the investment return on the cash you do not tie up in a down payment. Compare homes at the same household size and distance to the water.

## Protect your money
Check deposit protection by bank and ownership category with the FDIC estimator. Keep a return fund for lease exit and travel home.

## Housing help
HUD lists rental assistance programs and the contact for local public housing agencies. A HUD-approved housing counselor can help you compare renting and buying.
