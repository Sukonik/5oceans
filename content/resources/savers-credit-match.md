## Saver's Credit (2026)
Also called the Retirement Savings Contributions Credit, it rewards low- and moderate-income workers who contribute to an IRA or workplace plan. For 2026, the income limits are:
- **$40,250** for single filers and married individuals filing separately.
- **$60,375** for heads of household.
- **$80,500** for married couples filing jointly.

The credit is a percentage of eligible contributions, and the percentage drops as income rises. Check IRS instructions for the exact amount for your income.

## Saver's Match (starting with 2027 contributions)
Under federal law, eligible savers may receive a matching contribution of up to **$1,000 per person**, depending on eligible contributions, income and other rules. Confirm the year, your eligibility and the receiving account on the IRS Saver's Match page.

> Keep a future match out of cash available to spend today. [Sea Pig](animal:sea-pig) shows only money you can count on.

## Using it in a plan
- Start with a small, automatic deposit to an [IRA](resource:roth-ira) from each paycheck.
- Check the credit when you file for the tax year of the contribution.
- Re-check eligibility every year because the limits change.
