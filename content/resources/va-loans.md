## What they are
VA loans are made by private lenders and partly guaranteed by the VA. The guarantee lets lenders offer terms most buyers cannot get: often **no down payment** and **no monthly mortgage insurance**.

## Who is eligible
Eligibility depends on your service history and discharge status. Eligible groups include many veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and some surviving spouses. You request a **Certificate of Eligibility** through the VA or your lender. The VA sets the details.

## The funding fee
Most borrowers pay a one-time funding fee, which can be financed into the loan. For a first-time use purchase, the VA's published schedule is:
- **Less than 5% down:** 2.15%
- **5% to 9.99% down:** 1.50%
- **10% or more down:** 1.25%

The fee is higher for repeat use with little down, and different for refinances. Veterans with a service-connected disability rating of **10% or more** are generally exempt. Confirm the current chart on VA.gov.

## Credit and income
The VA does not set a minimum credit score. Lenders set their own and also look at debt-to-income and the VA's residual-income test.

## Occupancy
The home must be your primary residence, generally within a reasonable time after closing. A VA loan can finance a one-to-four-unit property if you live in one unit.
