## What they are
The USDA's Single Family Housing Guaranteed Loan program backs loans made by approved lenders for homes in eligible rural areas. Borrowers can often finance 100% of the appraised value, so no down payment is required.

## Who and where
- **Location:** the home must be in an eligible rural area. Many suburban areas near cities qualify. Check the address on USDA's eligibility map.
- **Income:** household income must be within the program limit, which USDA lists as up to 115% of the area median income.
- **Occupancy:** the home must be your primary residence, and a modest home for the area.
- **Credit:** lenders set their own minimums.

## Fees
USDA's published fees for the guaranteed program are a **1% upfront guarantee fee**, which is financed into the loan, and a **0.35% annual fee** charged monthly on the balance. Fees can change each fiscal year, so confirm the current figures with a lender.

## Other USDA help
- **Direct loans** for very low and low-income buyers in rural areas are a separate program with its own limits.
- **Home repair loans and grants (Section 504)** help eligible homeowners fix health and safety hazards. Grants are available for eligible owners aged 62 or older. See [Aging in place](animal:swan) and the [Programs guide](Programs.md#housing-and-care-support).
