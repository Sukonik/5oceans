## What it is
A duplex (or triplex or fourplex) has multiple legal units on one lot. You live in one and rent the others. This is the main version of [Hermit Crab](animal:hermit-crab): a home that helps pay for itself.

## The numbers (fictional example)
- Carrying cost before rent: **$3,300** a month.
- Rent at full occupancy: **$2,000**. After 95% occupancy and 8% management, **$1,748** is available.
- Owner still pays **$1,552** a month, compared with **$2,550** for an owner-only home. That is **$998** a month less, before income tax.
- If the duplex needs **$60,000** more cash up front than the owner-only home, the simple recovery time is about **60 months**, excluding taxes, time value and resale. Use the extra cash, not the whole purchase price, in that comparison.
- Principal paydown is already in the payment. It is not extra spendable income.

## Check before you buy
- **Legal use:** certificate of occupancy or equivalent, and zoning that permits the number of units.
- **Rents:** independent comparisons, not the seller's figure.
- **Financing:** ask lenders how they count projected rent and what owner-occupancy requires. Loans for an owner-occupied two-to-four-unit home are often available with different terms than an investor loan.
- **Insurance:** a landlord or owner-occupied multi-unit policy, plus flood quotes if the property is near water.
- **Taxes:** allocate shared expenses between your unit and the rented unit on a supportable basis, and expect a property reassessment after purchase.

## Risks
Vacancy, a problem tenant, a repair that hits both units, and a rise in rates if the loan adjusts. Hold a reserve and test a bad year.

## Alternatives
If you already own a lot, building an [ADU](resource:adu) may cost less than buying a second property. If you do not want to be a landlord, compare with [renting](resource:renting) and investing the difference.
