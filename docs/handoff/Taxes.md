# Taxes
Use after-tax household cash for lifestyle decisions and a separate worksheet for taxable income. Tax rules depend on year, residence, filing status, source and type of income. Keep federal and state columns separate. V1 should accept an estimate supplied by the visitor and show which assumptions it includes.

## Property and rent
Separate rental days at fair value, personal-use days, vacant days available to rent, and days before the unit is ready and available. Mixed-use homes can have expense allocation and deduction limits. In an owner-occupied duplex allocate shared expenses on a supportable basis. Track maintenance, capital improvements, mortgage interest, principal, depreciation and reserves as different categories. Principal and money set aside in a reserve account reduce available cash but generally are not current rental expense deductions. Depreciation can reduce taxable income without a current cash outflow. Keep the land value separate. [IRS Publication 527](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527)

A later sale may involve home-sale exclusion rules, allocation to a rental portion and tax on depreciation previously allowed or allowable. Avoid showing gross selling price as spendable retirement cash. Subtract debt payoff, selling costs and a tax estimate. [IRS Publication 523](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p523)

## Moving and travel
Royal Tern, Giant Squid and Wandering Albatross should record domicile assumptions, residences available, days in each place and jurisdiction of income. New York domicile and statutory-residency tests are separate; a day count alone does not settle domicile. US citizens and resident aliens abroad generally report worldwide income to the US. Local residence, treaty treatment and available credits or exclusions need country-specific review. Link that country’s tax authority before publishing a city-specific estimate. [NY resident definitions](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/file/pit_definitions.htm) · [IRS international filing guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad)

**Display rule.** If tax is unknown, show before-tax and a blank tax input. A missing estimate is never an automatic zero. [After-tax balance checks](Investments.md#spendable-balances)
