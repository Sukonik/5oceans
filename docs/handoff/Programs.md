# Programs
Use these as discovery cards. A card needs a trigger, eligibility question, official owner, and one action. Keep the amount pending until the visitor supplies a statement or confirms eligibility. Coverage here is US-focused, with New York examples; international destination pages require local research.

## Governmental 457b
**Best entry points:** Largemouth Bass for saving, Yeti Crab for drawing, Spider Monkey for dates. Ask whether the employer plan is governmental, who administers it, whether the person actually separates, and whether any balance came from another plan. A governmental 457(b) generally permits distributions after severance; native funds generally escape the federal 10% additional early-distribution tax. Ordinary federal income tax generally applies to pretax draws. Rolled-in amounts and designated Roth distributions require separate treatment. Reduced hours with the same employer may leave access unchanged. Confirm the earliest permitted payment, schedule and tax treatment in the plan documents. Moving assets to an IRA can change early-access treatment. [IRS early-distribution exceptions](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-exceptions-to-tax-on-early-distributions) · [New York State Deferred Compensation Plan](https://www.nysdcp.com/)

## New York ERS
**Best entry points:** Pink River Dolphin for the work decision, Colossal Squid for lifetime income, Giant Otter for survivor questions. NYSLRS is the system; ERS is the Employees’ Retirement System. Ask for tier, specific plan, credited service, vesting, pensionable earnings, work-stop date, claim date and payment option. Compare two official estimates at the same claim age, one with service and earnings frozen at departure. Then vary claim age. Verify part-time credit, any reduction and employer retiree-health rules separately. Do not substitute a full-career estimate for a midcareer exit. [NYSLRS eligibility and Retirement Online instructions](https://www.osc.ny.gov/retirement/members/are-you-vested-and-what-it-means)

## Social Security
Enter the intended last year of covered work and claiming age. The retirement formula generally uses the highest 35 years; fewer earnings years can reduce the amount. Compare household income while both partners live with the survivor scenario. Obtain SSA estimates directly, and record which future earnings they assume. [SSA on stopping work](https://www.ssa.gov/benefits/retirement/planner/stopwork.html) · [SSA retirement planning](https://www.ssa.gov/prepare/plan-retirement) · [SSA survivors](https://www.ssa.gov/benefits/survivors/)

## IRAs and family saving
Rhesus Macaque Monkey should show two owners and two balances. Married couples filing jointly may qualify for a spousal IRA contribution based on combined taxable compensation. Verify compensation, annual combined traditional/Roth limit per person, Roth income rules and traditional IRA deductibility. Brokerage brand belongs in the provider field. For Green Anaconda, track regular Roth IRA contributions, conversions and earnings separately before assigning accessible dollars. [IRS contribution rules](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-ira-contribution-limits) · [IRS distribution rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b)

## Saving incentives
Sea Pig checks the federal Saver’s Credit for 2026 and the Saver’s Match beginning with 2027 contributions. For the latter, eligible savers may receive up to $1,000 per person based on eligible contributions, income and other rules. Confirm the year, eligibility and participating receiving account. Keep a future match out of cash available to spend today. [IRS Saver’s Match](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/savers-match)

## Self-employed and specialized
| Program | Who should see it | First action |
|---|---|---|
| One-participant 401(k) | Black Seadevil visitors with net self-employment earnings and no eligible employees other than a spouse | Check the employee census, net earned income and interaction with other workplace deferrals. [IRS owner-only plan](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/one-participant-401k-plans) |
| Federal Employees Retirement System | Federal civilian employees choosing Colossal Squid | Enter basic benefit, Social Security and TSP separately; use the federal retirement system in place of a New York ERS estimate. [OPM FERS](https://www.opm.gov/retirement-center/fers-information/) |
| Military Savings Deposit Program | Eligible service members on qualifying deployments | Check deployment eligibility and the $10,000 interest-bearing deposit cap. | Retirement Savings Lost and Found | Visitors who changed private-sector employers | Search prior-employer plans and verify any result directly with the administrator. [DOL search](https://lostandfound.dol.gov/) |

## Housing and care support
**USDA Section 504:** An applicant generally must own and occupy the home, have very low income, live in an eligible rural area and be unable to obtain affordable credit elsewhere. Grants for eligible owners aged 62 or older address health and safety hazards. Ask the local USDA office about eligible work, funding and repayment conditions. Use with Swan and Hippopotamus. [USDA repair assistance](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-repair-loans-grants)

**Medicare Savings Programs:** Visitors approaching Medicare with limited income or resources should check state-administered premium or cost-sharing help. Ask the state Medicaid office; a SHIP counselor can explain choices. Use with Swan, Giant Otter or Spider Monkey. [Medicare eligibility guide](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/help/medicare-savings-programs) · [Find a SHIP counselor](https://www.shiphelp.org/)

**Housing counseling and ADU financing:** Hermit Crab should route an owner considering a second unit to a housing counselor and to eligible financing options. The local zoning determination and lender decision are separate gates. [Rental feasibility](RealEstate.md#buy-build-or-rent) · [Fannie Mae ADU options](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/originating-underwriting/mortgage-products/accessory-dwelling-units) · [HUD counseling](https://www.hud.gov/counseling)

**Medical savings route.** Visitors with eligible health coverage can check HSA contribution eligibility and qualified medical reimbursements. [HSA access checks](Investments.md#spendable-balances) · [IRS HSA rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969)
