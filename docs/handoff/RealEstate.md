# Real estate
## Buy, build or rent
**Primary animal: Hermit Crab.** Keep its approved housing theme and offer a “Let a rental offset my housing cost” option. Use Royal Tern for seasonal rentals and Giant Squid for the combined cost of two homes. This adds a property strategy within the existing roster.

| Route | What to price | Eligibility or evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Buy an existing legal duplex; live in one unit | Purchase, closing, inspection, work, debt service, tax reassessment, owner share, rents and reserves | Certificate of occupancy or equivalent, legal use, independent rent comparisons, lender terms, liability and flood quotes |
| Build or convert a lawful ADU on an owned lot | Design, survey, engineering, permits, utilities, site work, construction, contingency, carrying interest and lease-up | Confirm parcel zoning, setbacks, floodplain rules, permits, occupancy approval, lender treatment of projected rent and any rental restrictions. Policies differ by locality. [Fannie Mae ADU options](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/originating-underwriting/mortgage-products/accessory-dwelling-units) |
| Buy land and build a rental house | Land, utility connections, design, build, contingency, loan draws, interest, inspections, conversion to permanent financing and lease-up | Obtain construction financing terms, zoning and a usable occupancy date. Use zero rent during design and construction. Confirm property investment terms with the lender. |
| Buy a house solely to rent it out | Acquisition costs and full operating budget plus vacancy, financing and reserves | Investment-property underwriting and insurance. HUD 203(k) rehabilitation financing generally targets eligible owner-occupied existing homes; enter an investor loan quote for a dedicated rental. [HUD FHA 203(k)](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/203k/203k--df) |
| Keep the current home or rent nearby | Rent, utilities, insurance, transport and investable cash retained | Compare all routes at the same household size, ownership period and distance to the water. [Find a HUD housing counselor](https://www.hud.gov/counseling) |

**Financing toggle.** Let the visitor choose cash purchase, mortgage, or construction-to-permanent financing. In cash mode enter the full acquisition or construction outlay up front and zero debt service. Continue to include taxes, insurance, vacancy, repairs and the opportunity cost of committed cash in the comparison. Compare that capital with the amount otherwise available for the age-45 bridge.

## Worked example
Illustrative legal owner-occupied duplex, fictional rents and financing, before income tax. The owner occupies one unit year-round and offers the other for a long-term lease. The comparison baseline is a suitable owner-only home costing $2,550 a month. Replace every line with a local quote or estimate.

| Monthly item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Mortgage principal and interest | $2,100 |
| Property tax | $600 |
| Home and landlord cover | $180 |
| Flood insurance | $100 |
| Owner-paid common utilities | $120 |
| Maintenance and capital reserve | $200 |
| **Total carrying cost before rent** | **$3,300** |
| Scheduled monthly rent at full occupancy | $2,000 |
| Rent collected at 95% economic occupancy | $1,900 |
| Management at 8% of collected rent | $152 |
| **Rent available toward the $3,300** | **$1,748** |
| **Owner’s remaining monthly cost** | **$1,552** |

Rent covers about **53%** of carrying costs. Relative to the $2,550 comparison home, the duplex saves **$998 a month** before income tax. If the duplex requires $60,000 more cash up front than the comparison home, the simple recovery time is about **60 months**. This excludes time value, taxes and resale. Use incremental cash, not the entire purchase price, for this comparison. Principal paydown is already in debt service and does not become additional spendable income.

**Can rent cover the whole property?** With these assumptions, gross monthly rent required is $3,300 ÷ (0.95 × 0.92) = **$3,776**, rounded up. At the assumed $2,000 rent this property needs an owner contribution. For an entirely rented property, sum achievable rents for all legal units and rerun every expense with the appropriate financing and insurance.

**Bad year.** At 75% occupancy the $2,000 scheduled rent yields $1,500; after $120 management, $1,380 offsets carrying costs. Owner cost rises to $1,920 a month. Add a separate $6,000 uninsured loss and the annual outlay becomes **$29,040**, or $2,420 a month on average. Show the actual loss month as well as the average. Deduct reserves available to pay a shock only once.

**Build delay.** Six extra months at $1,400 monthly construction carrying cost consumes another **$8,400** before the first permitted tenancy. Track the months without rent in the actual timeline. Show both the cash needed at the worst point and stabilized annual cash flow. Comparing solely on the first fully rented year hides this risk.

## Seasonal use
Royal Tern should compare legal long-term tenancy with seasonal leasing based on locally verified permitted use, minimum term, booking costs, taxes, management, personal-use days and periods the owner returns. Model the property sitting empty as a third case. For a Caribbean winter, price the second residence, flights and home monitoring. Verify HOA, mortgage and policy restrictions before treating any revenue as feasible. [IRS rental and personal use](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527) · [Insurance](Insurance.md#recreation-and-liability)

## Four-season homes
Hippopotamus compares cabin conversion with purchasing a year-round home. Enter winter access, insulation, heating, power backup, water and septic, contractor bids, property tax and moving costs. Compare the loan estimate by term, APR, closing cash and monthly payment. Verify rehabilitation eligibility against the existing building. For qualifying rural owner-occupants, check repair assistance. [CFPB Loan Estimate](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/loan-estimate/) · [HUD rehabilitation program](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/203k/203k--df) · [USDA Section 504](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-repair-loans-grants)

## Shared ownership
Capybara runs a missed-payment season and a co-owner buyout. Record who holds title, who owes debt, each cash contribution, permitted rental use, occupancy calendar and repair obligations. Request independent legal review of ownership, exit rights and local rules before treating an agreement as settled. Show the surviving owners’ monthly cost after a buyout and test whether the reserve can fund it. [Tax worksheet](Taxes.md#property-and-rent)

## City-page evidence
For each address or illustrative neighborhood add the municipal zoning authority, rental registration office, floodplain administrator, assessor, local rental comparables and dated insurance quotes. Use “unverified” until a source confirms the assumption. Long Beach, NY is a proposed first city. This handoff makes no determination that a particular Long Beach parcel permits an ADU, duplex or seasonal rental.
