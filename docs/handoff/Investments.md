# Investments
First ask when the visitor intends to spend the money. A Roth IRA, governmental 457(b) or taxable brokerage is an account arrangement. A bill, bond or fund is something the account may hold. Track the account’s access rules separately from the investment’s market and liquidity risks.

## Cash and Treasuries
| Vehicle | Good discovery trigger | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Insured bank savings | West Indian Manatee needs cash available immediately after a layoff | Use EDIE for the bank, depositor and ownership category. Verify the institution, other deposits and withdrawal access. [FDIC EDIE](https://edie.fdic.gov/) |
| Treasury bill ladder | Black Bear knows the months income stops; Blue Whale plans a year away | Bills mature in 4 to 52 weeks. Match maturities to bills due, leave immediate cash outside the ladder, and model reinvestment rates changing. Early sale may change proceeds. [Treasury bill terms](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/treasury-bills/) |
| Series I savings bonds | A reserve has a portion that can stay untouched for at least a year | Generally locked for 12 months. Redemption before five years normally forfeits three months of interest. Exclude newly purchased bonds from first-year emergency cash. [TreasuryDirect I bonds](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/savings-bonds/i-bonds/) |
| Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities | A visitor wants inflation-linked principal for a known later need | Compare maturity date with spending date. Market value can fall before maturity. Check taxation of annual principal increases in taxable accounts. [TreasuryDirect TIPS](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/tips/) |

## Spendable balances
For every balance, capture owner, account type, liquid amount, earliest access date, expected taxes, market risk, and purpose. If tax is already reflected in the entered spendable balance, skip the extra haircut. Keep a reserve earmarked for a house repair out of the retirement bridge.

| Route | Example animal | Access check |
|---|---|---|
| Taxable brokerage with diversified funds | Largemouth Bass; Green Anaconda | Accessible while working, but sale proceeds depend on market value and taxes. Distinguish dividends from total return; model price declines and distributions in the same portfolio. [SEC on diversification](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation) |
| Roth IRA regular contribution basis | Green Anaconda | Track lifetime regular contributions less amounts already withdrawn. IRS ordering rules distinguish regular contributions, conversions and earnings. [IRS Publication 590-B](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b) |
| Governmental 457(b) after separation | Yeti Crab | Confirm the plan permits the requested distribution and separately review rolled-in amounts. Hours reductions and employer separation are different scenarios. [IRS exceptions](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-exceptions-to-tax-on-early-distributions) |
| HSA | Spider Monkey; Giant Manta Ray | Verify coverage and eligibility to contribute. Qualified unreimbursed medical spending can be tax-free; premiums qualify only under specific exceptions. Keep receipts and exclude expenses already reimbursed. [IRS Publication 969](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969) |

## Comparing an investment with a rental
Compare spendable cash, time required, concentration, liquidity and downside on the same starting equity. A rental scenario includes financing and upkeep as well as rent. Count cash flow, principal paydown and appreciation separately. In a portfolio scenario, avoid adding dividends to a return assumption that already includes them.
