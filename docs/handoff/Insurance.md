# Insurance
Coverage belongs to a person, property and policy period. Link each figure to a dated quote or mark it as estimated. A flood map, a broker’s indication and a policy declaration answer different questions.

## Waterfront cover
Use with Peacock Flounder, Painted Turtle, Goblin Shark, and Red Cushion Sea Star. For each address request separate figures for home or condo coverage, wind or named-storm deductible, flood building and contents cover, exclusions, and temporary living costs. Confirm whether a deductible is a fixed amount or a percentage and what amount it applies to. Ask about basement limitations, accessory structures, and condo master policy versus an individual policy. Ordinary homeowners policies generally exclude flood. Standard NFIP policies generally exclude additional living expenses and lost rent. Price displacement and rental interruption separately. [FloodSmart](https://www.floodsmart.gov/) · [NFIP covered and excluded losses](https://www.floodsmart.gov/whats-covered) · [Flood zones and maps](https://www.floodsmart.gov/flood-zones-and-maps)

| Record | Example field | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Hazard evidence | Map source and effective date; history of access-road flooding | Compare sites and request quotes. Price is set using additional factors. |
| Policy quote | Annual premium, insured value, deductible, limit, effective date and occupancy type | Divide recurring premium by 12 in the baseline. Deductible goes into the shock scenario once. |
| Exposure | Nights displaced, reasonable nightly cost, stored contents and interrupted rent | Subtract only benefits documented as payable for this event. Use zero for unverified reimbursement in the stress case. |

## Leaving work
Collect four dates: last day worked, actual end of employer coverage, earliest replacement start, and first pension deposit. Confirm dependent coverage and any employer retiree-health eligibility in writing. For federal COBRA where applicable, confirm the plan’s notice, premium, election deadline, coverage period and retroactive premium due. Compare with the Marketplace estimate for the household’s expected annual income. In New York use NY State of Health. Consider network, deductible and prescriptions as well as premium. [DOL COBRA overview](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/cobra) · [Marketplace coverage for retirees](https://www.healthcare.gov/retirees/) · [NY special enrollment](https://nystateofhealth.ny.gov/specialenrollment.html)

## Travel and care
Giant Manta Ray should ask where each person will physically receive care. A US policy may have a limited network and Original Medicare generally covers care outside the US only in limited situations. Price foreign or travel coverage, exclusions, trip duration and evacuation separately. Swan and Giant Otter should distinguish skilled services from ongoing custodial help. Medicare generally does not cover most long-term custodial care. Compare paid help, family capacity and possible state programs. [Medicare abroad](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/travel-outside-the-u.s.) · [Medicare long-term care](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/long-term-care) · [Medicare Savings Programs](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/help/medicare-savings-programs)

## Recreation and liability
Pike should ask for a separate boat and dock quote, insured value, storm haul-out costs and seasonal exclusions. Hermit Crab and Royal Tern should verify owner-occupied versus landlord cover, long-term versus short-term tenants, liability, vacancy clauses and loss-of-rent cover with a licensed insurer. A change in use can change the policy. During construction, ask about builder’s risk and the transition to occupied coverage. Obtain written quotes before counting any insurance recovery.
